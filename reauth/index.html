        Confidentiality and HIPAA agreement

        This tool handles protected health information (PHI) of Trust Home Care patients. By using it, you agree that you will:

        1. Use it only for Trust Home Care patients you are assigned to, and only for authorization work.
        2. Use the minimum necessary information: upload only the files a request needs.
        3. Not save, copy, screenshot, print or send patient information outside the approved work systems (Axxess, the agency email and this tool), and not use personal devices or personal email accounts for patient information.
        4. Keep your login private, lock your screen when you step away, and work where others cannot see your screen.
        5. Check every form, email and note before sending, and send patient information only to the payer it is meant for.
        6. Report any mistake that may expose patient information (wrong recipient, lost file, someone else using your login) right away to your supervisor at support@cloverva.org.
        7. Understand that this work is covered by HIPAA and by the Business Associate Agreement between Trust Home Care and Clovers Virtual Assistance, and that breaking these rules can lead to loss of access and disciplinary action.
